Amin Saifan

I’m a first-year UCL BASc student concentrating in Quantitative & Applied Economics. I co-founded Illustra, an AI-native learning system, and work across economics, mathematics, data science, and technology. I’m interested in how people make decisions and how new tools change what they can do.

THE BIGGEST PROBLEM I SEE

From 45% to 100%.

Today, we spend roughly 45% of our time on screens; the only moments we are truly out of reach are when we sleep or shower. Wearable technology can close that gap by putting the display directly in front of our eyes. With information and interfaces always available, we can outsource low-value tasks and interact with the digital world more seamlessly. The result: faster economic growth and a new stage in how humans extend their own capabilities.

Behind the build+

Illustra began as an idea: one place to write, organise, and learn from notes. An early version placed third at the Cambridge Startup Competition. We then pivoted and rebuilt.

The product now includes handwriting-to-text, AI-enhanced notes, flashcards, materials you can query, and linked pages. It is live with its first public cohort.

My role covers product research, development, and technical architecture, from the first concept through launch.
